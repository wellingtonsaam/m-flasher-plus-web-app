  What this page can and can't do
  
        
    
      • On a unit running normal firmware: Check FM-1 status starts a small helper program through the firmware's own update route. That writes a few KiB to the area firmware keeps for updates, then the unit runs the helper from RAM. When you finish, or after 5 minutes without this page asking anything, it goes back to its firmware.
        • 
    
      • With m-bootloader: hold PLAY (SEQ PLAY on the SMK-37) at power-on, choose MIDI MODE, and press Check FM-1 status. That works with no firmware at all, and writes nothing until you ask.
        • 
    
      • Backups are the whole flash, byte for byte, in the same format as the FirmWave app on a computer, and checked with a CRC the chip computes. They go to your Downloads. Keep them safe.
        • 
    
      • Restoring covers firmware, settings and presets. The bootloader and key area are never written from here, nor the SMK-37's calibration and Bluetooth data.
        • 
    
      • You still need the FirmWave app on a computer for a unit that won't start and has no m-bootloader, for download mode, and to put the stock bootloader back. Get FirmWave.
